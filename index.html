Kakashi Hatake

The legendary Copy Ninja of Naruto. Known for his calm attitude, tactical brilliance, signature mask, and lightning-fast Chidori, Kakashi mentors Naruto, Sasuke, and Sakura as the leader of Team 7.

A skilled shinobi who values teamwork and loyalty, Kakashi combines clever strategy with powerful ninjutsu to protect his friends and village.

📺 About Naruto

Naruto takes place in a world of hidden ninja villages, chakra-based abilities, and dangerous missions. Young ninja train under experienced leaders to develop their skills and protect their communities.

Kakashi becomes Team 7's teacher, challenging his students to understand that teamwork matters more than individual glory.

🥷 Character Profile

Village: Konohagakure (Hidden Leaf)

Team: Team 7

Specialties: Lightning Release, Sharingan, tactics, ninjutsu

Personality: Relaxed, observant, loyal, and surprisingly humorous

Signature move: Chidori / Lightning Blade

⚔️ Skills & Abilities

⚡ Chidori

High-speed lightning chakra concentrated in the hand for a devastating close-range strike.

👁️ Sharingan

Helps Kakashi read movements and copy many techniques; using it consumes significant chakra.

🐺 Summoning Jutsu

Calls a pack of ninja hounds to track targets and assist with missions.

🌍 Earth Style

Creates defensive barriers and manipulates terrain to control the battlefield.

🌊 Water Style

Uses water-based techniques for offense, defense, and adaptability.

🧠 Tactical Genius

Analyzes opponents and builds strategies even when outmatched.
