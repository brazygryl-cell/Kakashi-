KAKASHI WEBSITE

1. Extract the ZIP file.
2. Open index.html in Chrome, Edge, or Firefox.
3. Click Play Kakashi’s Theme to start music (browsers require a click).
4. Click Activate Chidori to play the included sound and see a lightning flash.
5. Character images use external URLs and require an internet connection. They may fail if the source blocks hotlinking.

Audio files are included in assets/.
